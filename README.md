### Learning & Listening

* [BBC Radio 3](https://www.bbc.co.uk/radio3) - A premier source for curated classical music broadcasts, composer documentaries, and live concert recordings.
* [Naxos](https://www.naxos.com/) - One of the largest classical music catalogs online, offering recordings, reference articles, and educational resources.
* [Classics for Kids](https://www.classicsforkids.com/) - An accessible introduction to classical music with composer biographies, games, and beginner-friendly lessons.
* [The Kennedy Center: Explore Classical Music](https://www.kennedy-center.org/education/resources-for-educators/classroom-resources/media-and-interactives/) - High-quality educational materials and interactive media for understanding classical music history and performance.
* [Classic FM](https://www.classicfm.com/) - A popular and approachable platform for discovering famous works, composers, and modern classical recommendations.

### Scores, Research & Advanced Exploration

* [IMSLP](https://imslp.org/) - The world’s largest free public-domain sheet music archive for performers, students, and researchers.
* [Classical Archives](https://www.classicalarchives.com/) - A deeply organized classical music library with streaming recordings, composer data, and curated playlists.
* [Musopen](https://musopen.org/) - A valuable resource offering free recordings, sheet music, and music theory materials without copyright restrictions.
* [Oxford Music Online](https://www.oxfordmusiconline.com/) - An authoritative scholarly reference platform featuring Grove Music Online and extensive academic articles.
* [Gramophone](https://www.gramophone.co.uk/) - The leading classical music review magazine for expert critiques of recordings, artists, and performances.
